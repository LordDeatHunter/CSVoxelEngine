# Biomes

The world has seven biomes. **Ocean** is a flooded basin that ramps from a sandy shore down to bare stone. The six land biomes sit on a 3×3 grid of temperature and humidity:

|               | wet    | semi-dry | dry    |
|---------------|--------|----------|--------|
| **cold**      | taiga  | taiga    | frozen |
| **temperate** | forest | steppe   | steppe |
| **warm**      | forest | savanna  | desert |

Borders blend smoothly, so a biome change is a slope rather than a cliff. Sand and snow are always kept far apart: the band between them is forest or steppe.

| Biome | What it is like |
|---|---|
| Forest | Rolling grass hills with flat valleys. Oak woods, flowers in open clumps, grass tufts, fallen logs and boulders. |
| Desert | Dunes of sand over sandstone. Cactus, dead trees, dead shrubs, wells and oases with palms. |
| Taiga | A snowy spruce wood. |
| Frozen | A tundra: permafrost under snow, sparse dead shrubs, leafless snags, occasional ice spikes. |
| Steppe | A dry, open grass plain with scattered dead trees and flowers. |
| Savanna | Dry grassland with umbrella-crowned stone pines and wild cotton. |
| Ocean | Sand, then gravel, then stone with depth. Sea stacks near shores, and icebergs drift where it is cold. |

## Climate effects

- Grass and leaves are **tinted by climate**: cold and dry grass is paler and yellower, warm and wet is lusher.
- **Snow and ice** appear as a column cools. Cold sea, lakes and rivers freeze into a walkable sheet whose edge breaks into floes and open leads. Mountain peaks wear a snow cap even in warm biomes.
- The screen is gently tinted warm over the desert and cool over snow, and the **aurora** shows in the night sky of cold lands.

## Terrain shape

On top of the climate, a relief layer lifts some regions into highlands with mountain ranges, rare massifs and lone peaks. Islands and archipelagos rise out of deep water. Ponds, rivers and rare **great lakes** fill the low ground, and ravines and caves cut below. Near the spawn the land is kept calm.

## Underground

Caves come as large rooms and winding tunnels, with rare entrances to the surface. Ores (coal, malachite, tin, hematite), gravel and sand pockets, geodes, glow mushrooms, stalactites, underground pools, hot springs and deep lava pockets are all found in the rock.
