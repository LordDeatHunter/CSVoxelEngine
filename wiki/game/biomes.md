# Biomes

The world has seven biomes. **Ocean** is a flooded basin that ramps from a sandy shore down to bare stone. The six land biomes sit on a 3×3 grid of temperature and humidity:

|               | wet    | semi-dry | dry    |
|---------------|--------|----------|--------|
| **cold**      | taiga  | taiga    | frozen |
| **temperate** | forest | steppe   | steppe |
| **warm**      | forest | savanna  | desert |

Borders blend smoothly, so a biome change is a slope rather than a cliff. Sand and snow are always kept far apart: the band between them is forest or steppe.

## Forest

Rolling grass hills with flat valleys. Oak woods, flowers in open clumps, grass tufts, fallen logs and boulders. The grass and leaves take their color from the climate.

## Steppe

An open, dry-tinted grass plain with scattered dead trees and flowers. Lakes and rivers hold water here just as in the forest.

## Savanna

Dry grassland of umbrella-crowned stone pines, with wild cotton growing in clumps.

## Desert

Dunes of sand over sandstone. Cacti, dead trees and dead shrubs, desert wells, and oases with palms. No surface water gathers here, and ponds close on their own rim before they reach it.

## Taiga

A snowy spruce wood, with snow on the ground and dusted over the canopy.

## Frozen

A tundra: permafrost under the snow, sparse dead shrubs, leafless snags and occasional ice spikes.

## Ocean

Sand, then gravel, then stone with depth, with sea stacks near shores. In cold seas the water freezes into a walkable sheet of ice whose edge breaks into floes and open leads, and icebergs drift where the sea is just cold enough.

## Where biomes meet

Borders bend and wander instead of tracing smooth blobs, and a coherent noise breaks the line between materials into ragged patches rather than a checkerboard of single columns.

## Climate effects

- Grass and leaves are **tinted by climate**: cold and dry grass is paler and yellower, warm and wet is lusher.
- **Snow and ice** appear as a column cools. Cold sea, lakes and rivers freeze into a walkable sheet. Mountain peaks wear a snow cap even in warm biomes.
- The screen is gently tinted warm over the desert and cool over snow.
- The **aurora** shows in the night sky of cold lands, and nowhere else.

## Underground

Caves come as large rooms and winding tunnels, with rare entrances to the surface. Ores (coal, malachite, tin, hematite), gravel and sand pockets, geodes, glow mushrooms, stalactites, underground pools, hot springs and deep lava pockets are all found in the rock.
