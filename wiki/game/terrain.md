# Terrain

On top of the climate, a relief layer shapes the land. All of it is a pure function of the world seed and position, so a given spot always looks the same.

## Highlands

A continent-scale lift raises some regions into highlands. In dry land a highland becomes a lifted plateau rather than broken country, and its rivers dry into washes. Near the spawn the land is kept calm, so highlands begin on the horizon.

## Mountains

Mountain ranges rise only inside highlands, as chains hundreds of blocks long. Rare **massifs** climb to an absolute summit with a pointed, spurred body, and a **lone peak** stands only where the land around it is flat. A high crest above the tree line is bare stone, and peaks wear a snow cap even in warm biomes.

## Islands

Islands and archipelagos rise out of deep water. Island land is ordinary land of its climate, sand-rimmed where it meets the sea, and islets string out in chains.

<ShotGallery slugs="island,island_beach,archipelago" />

## Great lakes and rivers

Far from the spawn, rare **great lakes** spread over lobed bowls a few hundred blocks across, sometimes with an island in the main lobe. A lake standing above sea level feeds a river that steps down one block at a time, so the river meets the lake at its own surface, and an outflow channel may leave the lake's low side.

## The night sky

The star field turns once a day, the moon goes through its phases, and at night the moon is the world's key light.
