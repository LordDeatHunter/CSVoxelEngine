# Layers: hell below, sky above

The overworld shares the world with two realms: a Nether-like hell under it and an Aether-like sky over it. There are no portals; you dig down or build up.

| Layer | Height (Y) | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| Sky islands | 320 to 420 | Floating islands everywhere, crowding into zones, with their own biomes, flora and mobs |
| Cloud sea | 290 to 301 | A thick, nearly continuous sea of solid cloud |
| Cloud patches | 198 to 203 | Solid cloud slabs above every mountain |
| Overworld | down to −300 | Everything the biomes page describes |
| Cave biomes | about −20 to −300 | The overworld's caves, echoing frozen and desert land near the top and turning into crystal, fungal and magma-touched caves below about −200 (see the [biomes page](/game/biomes)) |
| Barrier | −300 to −400 | A sealed band separating the overworld from hell |
| Hell | −401 and down | Endless cavern layers, with no bottom |

## Getting there

- **Down**: the barrier and hell's rock slabs can only be dug with an **iron pickaxe**, the top pickaxe tier. Hell itself has no bottom, and falls hurt as usual.
- **Up**: the sky has no gate. Solid cloud blocks catch a fall without damage, and the player sinks half a block into them, so you can build up and stand on them.
- **Back again**: once you have found a [waystone](/game/waystones) in a realm, you can travel to it from any other waystone.

## Hell

<ShotGallery slugs="hell_layer0" hero />

Hell is a stack of layers, each with a cavern pocket over an unbroken slab of hellrock. Deeper layers are more open and pond more lava. The sky is a red fog, with no sun, moon or stars.

| Biome | Look |
|---|---|
| Ashlands | Ash over cinder soil with scorched walls, ember blooms, and charred trees |
| Magma Caverns | Basalt and magma floors with lava pools |
| Fungal Grove | Fungal moss and glowing teal fungi, glow ferns, thick vines and fungal trees |

<ShotGallery slugs="hell_ashlands,hell_magma,hell_fungal,hell_layer1,hell_layer4" />

Hell is lit only by lava and glowing blocks. Its mobs are the **hellbrute**, a slow, tanky charging melee brute, and the **cinderwitch**, a caster that throws fire bolts that set you burning. Both grow tougher the deeper the layer.

## Sky

<ShotGallery slugs="sky_cloud_patches,sky_cloud_sea,sky_zone" />

Islands float everywhere in the band, so a flyer always has several in view, and crowd closer together inside zones. Each island is one of two biomes:

- **Meadow**: grass over dirt over stone, with tall grass, flowers, sparse vines and broad sky oaks.
- **Frost highland**: frosted turf over permafrost, with crystal outcrops, frost ferns, thicker vines and glowing crystal trees.

<ShotGallery slugs="sky_island,sky_meadow,sky_meadow_above,sky_frost,sky_frost_above" />

Its mobs are the diving **galeraptor**, a flyer that stoops at you, and the **cloud golem**, a slow bruiser whose slam sends out a shockwave. They spawn only inside zones; bigger zones hold harder mobs, and only the biggest zones hold golems.
