# Battle tower

A battle tower is a huge round stone tower, four to eight floors high and over forty blocks across, held by skeletons and
crowned by an altar that summons the [Skeleton King](/game/mobs/skeleton_king). Its walls cannot be mined, broken or blown
up: the only way to the top is up through every floor, and the only way to bring it down is to kill the king.

## Finding one

Battle towers are rare and never stand near the world spawn: expect at most one in a region about 1500 blocks across, a
long trip out. They rise on dry, fairly level land above the trees, so they are easy to see from a distance. In a desert
the tower is built of sandstone bricks and in the cold biomes of frosted bricks; the inside is the same everywhere.

## Floors

The entrance is at ground level. Inside the outer wall a stair corridor winds up the tower, and at every floor a doorway
leads off it into a round room. Each room is furnished (a hall, a library or barracks) and holds its own skeleton piles and
two chests.

The rooms are **dark**: the thick floors above shut out the sun, so only the lanterns that hang in them light the way.
Bring torches.

## Skeleton piles and sealed doors

Every floor has a few skeleton piles. When you come near one it raises a wave of
[skeletons](/game/mobs/skeleton), archers that shoot from a distance and rake up close, and after a couple of waves the
pile is spent. Higher floors raise stronger skeletons and more of them.

The stair to the next floor is barred by a portcullis. It opens by itself once every pile of the
floor below is spent and every skeleton it raised is dead. A skeleton that wanders off or vanishes is raised again from the
floor's piles, so it still has to be fought. Look at a pile or a sealed door to see how much of the floor is left.
Creative players who fly can pass, but they never wake the piles.

## Chests

Each floor has two tower chests, and one more waits on the roof beside the altar. The loot
gets better the higher you climb: supplies and metal on the low floors, then rarer gear, iron, scrolls and mana draughts,
with the best treasure on the roof.

## The altar and the Skeleton King

The altar stands in the middle of the roof. Right-click it once every floor door is open and
the ritual begins: a few seconds later the Skeleton King rises on top of it. The king is a boss with a health bar, three
phases and a temper that gets worse as it weakens; it slams the roof, fires volleys and calls skeletons to its side. It will
not stray far from its altar, and if everyone leaves it alone for a while it heals.

The king always drops one unique: the [Bone-breaker Blade](/game/items/bone_breaker_blade), the
[Bone Charm](/game/items/bone_charm) or the [Crystal Staff](/game/items/crystal_staff), together with bones, wrought iron,
bronze ingots and sometimes a scroll or a mana draught.

## The ruin

A few seconds after the Skeleton King falls, the tower collapses from the top down. What is left is a ruin: broken stumps of
wall, rubble heaps and the furnished ground floor, open to the sky. Anything still in a chest above the ground floor spills
out as the floor it stood on falls. The ruin is saved with the world and stays a ruin.

## Multiplayer

On a server the tower belongs to the world: every player sees the same doors open, the same king and the same ruin, and the
server decides when a floor is cleared and when the king is dead.
