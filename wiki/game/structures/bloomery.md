# Bloomery

<StructureViewer id="bloomery" />

## Properties

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Size | 2×2×2 |
| Ore capacity | 4 units |
| Fuel capacity | 4 units |

## How to build

Craft the bricks, stack them into a 2×2×2 cube and right-click a front face with a bare hand. The opening ends up facing you.

## Smelting

Load crushed iron and fuel through the opening and light it with a firestarter. The fire cools on its own; to keep it
hot, set a [Bellows](/game/blocks/bellows) against one of the two holes low in the side walls (the tuyeres) and land on
it: every landing blows the fire, the more the farther you fell, and any walking mob works it too. A fire too cold to
smelt slowly loses the progress of the ore it is working (the colder, the faster); there is no such thing as too hot.
Collect the blooms with an empty hand and forge them on an anvil: stack them, pick a recipe with a smithing hammer, then
hammer it out.
