# Mud Brick Kiln

<StructureViewer id="mud_brick_kiln" />

## Properties

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Size | 2×2×2 |
| Fuel capacity | 4 units |
| Melt speed | ×1 |
| Alloys metals | no |

## How to build

Craft the bricks, stack them into a 2×2×2 cube and right-click a front face with a bare hand. The opening ends up facing you.

## Melting

Right-click fuel into the mouth (it lies in a row at the back of the hearth), stand a crucible in front of it, charge
the crucible with anything that melts, and light the kiln with a firestarter. A lit kiln glows with a bed of embers on
its hearth and smokes from the flue on top of its dome. It keeps a crucible to one metal at a time (see the
[melting recipes](/game/reference/crafting#melting)).
