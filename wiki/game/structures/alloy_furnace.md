# Alloy Furnace

<StructureViewer id="alloy_furnace" />

## Properties

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Size | 3×3×3 |
| Fuel capacity | 8 units |
| Melt speed | ×1.5 |
| Alloys metals | yes |

## How to build

Craft the bricks, stack them into a 3×3×3 cube and right-click a front face with a bare hand. The opening ends up facing you.

## Melting and alloying

Right-click fuel into the firemouth (two rows of four on the firebox floor), stand a crucible on the pedestal in
the middle, charge it, and light the furnace with a firestarter. A lit furnace glows with a bed of embers across its
firebox and smokes from its chimney. It is the only kiln that takes the parts of an alloy together and turns them into
the alloy (see the [alloys](/game/reference/crafting#alloys)).
